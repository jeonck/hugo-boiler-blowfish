Every blog starts with a first post, and this is mine.

## Why I'm writing

I've been meaning to start a blog for years. The excuses pile up: not enough time, nothing interesting to say, who's going to read it anyway. But the real reason is simpler — I kept putting it off.

So here it is. A blog. Starting now.

## What to expect

This is a place for me to think out loud. Mostly about software development, but also about technology broadly, and whatever else catches my attention. Some posts will be tutorials. Some will be opinions. Some will just be notes to my future self.

I'm not writing for an audience. I'm writing because the act of writing forces clarity. If it turns out to be useful to someone else, that's a bonus.

## The setup

This site runs on [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) with the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme, deployed on GitHub Pages. Fast, free, and fully under my control — no platform dependency, no algorithm to game.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/jeonck). If you spot a typo or want to suggest something, feel free to open an issue.
